ZOMBIE CITY for Windows
=======================

How to play
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (your Desktop is fine).
  2. Open the "Zombie City" folder and double-click ZombieCity.exe.
  3. The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is new and not
     signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Controls
  Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, aim with the mouse, hold left click to fire, Space to dash,
  Q for the A-10, Esc to pause. A controller works too: plug it in or pair it and press a button.
  Change any key or button in Settings > Controls.
  F11 or Alt+Enter switches between full screen and a window.

Your progress (weapons, specimens, scores, settings) is saved on this PC.
Zombie City runs entirely from this folder. It is built on Electron (see LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html).
