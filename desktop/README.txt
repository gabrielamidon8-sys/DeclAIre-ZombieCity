ZOMBIE CITY LAUNCHER for Windows
================================

How to play
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (your Desktop is fine).
  2. Open the "Zombie City" folder and double-click "Zombie City Launcher.exe".
  3. The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is new and not
     signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  4. Press Play. When a new version of the game is out, the launcher offers it (a small download).

Wi-Fi co-op (up to 4 players)
  On the game's main menu choose Wi-Fi co-op, then Start hosting. Friends on the same Wi-Fi join
  from the Android app or their own launcher. If Windows asks about the firewall, allow Zombie City
  on private networks, or the others will not see your game.

Controls
  Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, aim with the mouse, hold left click to fire, Space to dash,
  Q for the A-10, Esc to pause. A controller works too: plug it in or pair it and press a button.
  Change any key or button in Settings > Controls.
  F11 or Alt+Enter switches between full screen and a window.

Website, news and downloads for phones: https://declairestudios.wordpress.com

Your progress (weapons, specimens, scores, settings) is saved on this PC.
Zombie City runs from this folder. It is built on Electron (see LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html).
