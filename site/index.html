One free account for Zombie City everywhere: in the browser, on Android and on Windows. Your weapons, dashes, specimens, bosses and best runs follow you to every device, and your name goes on the global board.

How your save is protected

  1. Your password is never stored.Wix, which runs the accounts, keeps only a one-way hash of it, so even we can't see it.
  2. Every account gets its own key.Your save is locked with a random 256-bit key made just for your account (AES-GCM).
  3. Only your password opens that key.The key itself is locked with your password (600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 with a salt of its own) before it leaves your device. Nobody else can read your save, not even DeclAIre Studios.

What your account does

  • Saves your progress on its own after every run.
  • Merges progress from every phone and PC you play on.
  • Puts your name and best run on the global board.
  • Lets your browser or phone remember your login, so you stay signed in.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

What we keep

Your email, the name you show on the board, your encrypted save, a short summary of it (best wave, runs, unlock counts) and your board scores. Nothing else: no location, no contacts, no ads, no tracking.

Where it lives

Accounts and saves are stored by Wix, which runs DeclAIre Studios accounts. Your password is handled by Wix and never seen by us. Your save is encrypted on your device with a key only your password opens.

Who can see what

Everyone can see your board name and scores. Only you can see your email and your save. We never sell or share your data.

Names on the board

A filter keeps slurs, swearing, threats and personal details (emails, phone numbers, links) out of names. It uses the open "List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene and Otherwise Bad Words" (CC BY 4.0), loaded from the jsDelivr CDN.

Age

You need to be 13 or older to make an account. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Delete everything

Log in and press Delete my account: your sign-in, your cloud save and your board entries are removed. Questions? Reach us through declairestudios.wordpress.com.
